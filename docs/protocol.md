# Study A protocol — draft v0.1

**Status:** Planning foundation dated 6 November 2026. Not institutionally approved and not ready to recruit. Protocol decisions below must be resolved and versioned before the affected activity.

## Design and scope

A single-institution requirements and design study at Mabini Colleges, Inc., informed by design science research, will combine local process investigation, a focused literature synthesis, requirements analysis, and formative walkthroughs. The design artifact is a workflow and implementation specification; a deployed portal feature is not required to answer A-RQ1–A-RQ3. See [research questions](introduction/research-questions.md).

## Participants and sampling

Candidate groups are eligible students, their participating guardians, Dean reviewers, and President's Office personnel in institutionally confirmed units. Confirm academic levels, whether minors are included, eligibility, recruitment arrangements, and consent requirements. Purposive selection should seek relevant role coverage and variation in device access, accessibility, and process experience. Justify and document the sample and stopping rationale from the study's information needs and feasibility; no count is fixed here. Report invitations, nonparticipation, and limitations.

## Planned procedure

1. Establish applicable research/data-access approval or institutional determination, consent arrangements, restricted storage, and permitted public outputs.
2. Verify literature sources and the requirements-derived current-process description against authorized local evidence. Record contradictions rather than assuming #740 proves actual local practice.
3. Interview/observe the confirmed roles, recording source type, permitted use, process variation, and unresolved needs. Separate observation from self-report.
4. Create current/proposed process maps, state transitions, screen mockups, and traceable requirements. Classify requirements as established constraints, design choices, or unresolved assumptions.
5. Dry-run instruments and mockups with synthetic cases. After applicable approval, conduct stakeholder walkthroughs covering normal requests, unclear terms, corrections, cancellation, staff review, printing, and access/device difficulties.
6. Record comprehension and design feedback, revise the design, and retain rejected suggestions with reasons. Confirm that material concerns are addressed or explicitly remain unresolved.
7. Freeze a versioned implementation handoff. Link Study B's measurement needs without representing its future baseline or outcomes as Study A findings.

## Analysis and evidence

Use a documented thematic/framework analysis for local needs and walkthrough feedback. Retain coding decisions, conflicting accounts, and revisions. Counts from small purposive groups describe only those participants. Link every final requirement to evidence or an explicit constraint and every anticipated benefit to a proposed mechanism and a Study B measure. Do not infer improvement from stakeholder agreement or a mockup walkthrough.

## Ethics and data management

Voluntary research participation is separate from service eligibility and payment-term acceptance. Determine applicable requirements for minors before recruitment. Collect only data needed for the questions. Set storage access, coding/linkage arrangements, retention/deletion periods, recording permission, quotation use, and disclosure review before collection. Keep real signatures, identifiable financial information, account identifiers, and linkage keys outside Git. Mockups and public examples must be synthetic. This protocol grants no permission to access records or recruit participants.

### Requesting and recording institutional decisions

The [institutional review request and decision record](appendices/institutional-review-package.md) provides proposed methods and safeguards, fields for unresolved local decisions, and an unfilled evidence record. Confirm the applicable institutional route and required forms, then complete a restricted submission copy with exact protocol, instrument, and mockup versions. The package is an unsubmitted draft; it supplies no institutional determination or participant consent.

After an actual determination or permission is issued, the accountable study lead must verify its authority, authenticity, covered activities/versions, dates, and conditions. Keep original attachments and personal details outside Git. Update the decision register below only with permitted, disclosure-reviewed metadata and non-sensitive evidence references. Missing, expired, out-of-scope, denied, or conditional permissions with unmet preconditions block the affected activity. Resolve consent/minors and data-management arrangements before the applicable recruitment, access, or collection; obtain each participant's research consent before their activity. A submission receipt or completed template does not release these gates.
