## Proposed request

We request a determination of the research review, participant protection, and data-access requirements applicable to Study A at Mabini Colleges, Inc. Please identify the authorized reviewers and record the permitted activities, conditions, versions, and validity period. Research review, office cooperation, access to records, and individual consent should be addressed separately where applicable; none is inferred from the portal implementation request.

The proposed study will investigate the current local process, derive evidence-supported requirements, and conduct formative walkthroughs of synthetic mockups. It seeks no change to financial eligibility or payment obligations. It does not authorize implementation or rollout. A deployed artifact is not needed for the proposed design walkthroughs. [Study B](https://github.com/frankkivenablao-mabini/guardian-signed-promissory-note-evaluation) owns operational comparison and implementation evaluation, with its own permissions and approved baseline before rollout.

## Proposed participant protections for review

- Research participation, refusal, skipped questions, and withdrawal must have no effect on grades, financial eligibility, service access, or approval of a promissory note. Confirm an invitation and session arrangement that makes this practical for students, guardians, and staff.
- Obtain separate, understandable research consent from each participant before their research activity. Guardian payment-term acceptance and student service requests are not research consent. A student's participation does not enroll a guardian, and a guardian's participation does not enroll a student. Resolve any additional minor-participant requirements before recruitment; no waiver or exemption is assumed.
- Provide the study purpose, tasks, expected burden, foreseeable confidentiality/discomfort risks, voluntary choices, approved researcher/complaint contacts, and intended outputs in the reviewed participant information. Do not promise a personal benefit, faster service, or absolute confidentiality. Confirm language and comprehension support.
- Propose notes as the default. Do not record audio/video, screens, or identifiable quotations unless specifically permitted and separately agreed by the participant. Explain any alternative for a person who declines recording; recording consent does not imply quotation or cross-study reuse consent.
- Use synthetic financial terms and signing examples. Do not ask a participant to show an account, make a real payment, sign a real financial undertaking, or supply a real financial signature for a walkthrough. Consent documentation, if signed, belongs in restricted storage too.
- Specify how to pause/stop a session, handle discomfort or accidental disclosure, and request withdrawal. Confirm the withdrawal contact, feasible deletion procedure, and cutoff after irreversible anonymization or aggregation before consent is sought; do not promise removal of data that can no longer be located.

**Unresolved:** Actual participant information/consent versions, contact roles, recording/quotation choices, minor arrangements, and withdrawal rules must be reviewed and recorded. These proposed protections are not completed consent or proof of operational compliance.
